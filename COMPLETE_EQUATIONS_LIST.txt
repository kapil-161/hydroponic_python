1. PHOTOSYNTHESIS MODEL (10 equations)
File: src/models/photosynthesis_model.py
=====================================================

1.1 ARRHENIUS TEMPERATURE RESPONSE
Rate(T) = Rate_25 * exp(Ea * (T_k - 298.15) / (298.15 * R * T_k))
- Description: Models the temperature dependency of enzyme kinetics (Vcmax, Jmax, Rd).

1.2 RUBISCO-LIMITED PHOTOSYNTHESIS RATE (Ac)
Ac = Vcmax * (Ci - Γ*) / (Ci + Kc * (1 + O2 / Ko))
- Description: Rate of photosynthesis when limited by the carboxylation capacity of RuBisCO.

1.3 LIGHT-LIMITED PHOTOSYNTHESIS RATE (Aj)
J = (I2 + Jmax - sqrt((I2 + Jmax)² - 4 * θ * I2 * Jmax)) / (2 * θ)
Aj = J * (Ci - Γ*) / (4 * (Ci + 2 * Γ*))
- Description: Rate of photosynthesis when limited by the rate of electron transport (J), which itself is dependent on light (I2).

1.4 NET PHOTOSYNTHESIS RATE (A_net)
A_net = min(Ac, Aj) - Rd
- Description: The net rate of CO2 assimilation, being the minimum of the Rubisco-limited and light-limited rates, minus mitochondrial respiration (Rd).

1.5 ENZYME SATURATION FACTOR (High LAI)
Factor = 1.0 - SaturationRate * (LAI - SaturationThreshold)
- Description: Reduces photosynthetic enzyme efficiency (Vcmax, Jmax) at high LAI due to self-shading and nitrogen dilution.

1.6 HOURLY CARBON ASSIMILATION (gC/m²/hr)
C_hourly = A_net * 3600 * umol_to_g_ratio
- Description: Converts the instantaneous rate (μmol CO2/m²/s) to an hourly carbon gain (g C/m²/hr).

1.7 TOTAL CANOPY PHOTOSYNTHESIS (gC/m² ground/hr)
Total_Assimilation = (Photosynthesis_sunlit * LAI_sunlit) + (Photosynthesis_shaded * LAI_shaded)
- Description: Calculates total canopy photosynthesis by summing the contributions from the sunlit and shaded portions of the canopy, scaled by their respective leaf area indices.

1.8 DAILY CARBON ASSIMILATION (gC/m² ground/day)
Daily_Assimilation = ∫(C_hourly(t) dt) from sunrise to sunset
- Description: The total carbon assimilated over the photoperiod, calculated by integrating the hourly rates.

1.9 DARK RESPIRATION
Net_Day_umol = A_net * photoperiod_seconds
Dark_Respiration_umol = Rd * dark_period_seconds
Net_umol_day = Net_Day_umol - Dark_Respiration_umol
- Description: Accounts for carbon loss due to respiration during the dark period.

1.10 TOTAL DAILY CARBON GAIN (gC/m²/day)
g_c_m2_day = Net_umol_day * umol_to_g_carbon_ratio
- Description: Final daily carbon gain per square meter of ground area after accounting for daytime assimilation and nighttime respiration.

2. RESPIRATION MODEL (15 equations)
File: src/models/respiration_model.py
=====================================================

2.1 Q10 TEMPERATURE FACTOR
Factor = Q10^((T - T_ref) / 10)
- Description: Standard temperature coefficient for modeling the rate of biological processes.

2.2 MAINTENANCE RESPIRATION (Rm)
Rm = BaseRate * Biomass * TempFactor * AgeFactor * N_Factor * TissueFactor * SizePenalty
- Description: Calculates the carbon cost of maintaining existing plant tissues.

2.3 AGE EFFECT ON RESPIRATION
AgeFactor = 1.0 + AgeCoefficient * max(0, Age_days)
- Description: Respiration rate increases with tissue age.

2.4 NITROGEN EFFECT ON RESPIRATION
N_Factor = 1.0 + N_Slope * (N_Ratio - 1.0)
- Description: Higher nitrogen content in tissues increases maintenance respiration costs.

2.5 SIZE PENALTY FACTOR
SizePenalty = 1.0 + PenaltyRate * (Biomass - Threshold)
- Description: Adds a respiration penalty for plants that grow beyond a certain biomass, reflecting increased transport and structural maintenance costs.

2.6 GROWTH RESPIRATION (Rg) - COMPOSITION-BASED
Rg = Σ(BiosyntheticCost_i * GrowthFraction_i * NewGrowth) * (1 - GrowthEfficiency)
- Description: Calculates respiration cost for new growth based on the biochemical composition (protein, lipid, etc.) of the new tissue.

2.7 TEMPERATURE ACCLIMATION
T_ref_new = T_ref_old + AcclimationRate * (T_avg_recent - T_ref_old)
- Description: The reference temperature for respiration acclimates to the recent average temperature.

2.8 HIGH-TEMPERATURE DAMAGE
Factor = Factor * exp(-DecayFactor * (T - T_max_threshold))
- Description: Reduces respiration at very high temperatures due to enzyme denaturation.

2.9 DIURNAL RESPIRATION FACTOR
Factor = Base + Amp1*sin(2π*(h-Peak1)/24) + Amp2*sin(2π*(h-Peak2)/24)
- Description: Models the circadian rhythm of respiration, often with peaks in the morning and afternoon.

2.10 TEMPERATURE STRESS FACTOR (Respiration)
Factor = 1.0 + StressFactor * (TempDeviation - Threshold)
- Description: Extreme temperatures (both high and low) can increase respiration due to stress responses.

2.11 RESPIRATORY QUOTIENT (RQ)
RQ = CO2_produced / O2_consumed
- Description: Varies based on the substrate being respired (e.g., ~1.0 for carbs, ~0.7 for lipids).

2.12 HOURLY RESPIRATION
R_hourly = R_daily / 24 * DiurnalFactor * DayNightFactor * TempStressFactor
- Description: Adjusts the daily average respiration rate for the specific hour based on diurnal rhythms, day/night cycles, and acute temperature stress.

2.13 TOTAL RESPIRATION
R_total = MaintenanceRespiration + GrowthRespiration
- Description: The total carbon lost to respiration is the sum of maintaining existing tissue and building new tissue.

2.14 GLUCOSE TO CARBON CONVERSION
C_respired = Glucose_respired * Glucose_to_C_ratio
- Description: Converts glucose consumed during respiration to the mass of carbon released.

2.15 CARBON TO CO2 CONVERSION
CO2_released = C_respired * C_to_CO2_ratio
- Description: Converts mass of carbon respired to mass of CO2 released.

3. PHENOLOGY MODEL (15 equations)
File: src/models/phenology_model.py
=====================================================

3.1 THERMAL TIME (GROWING DEGREE DAYS - GDD)
- BelowOpt: TT = (T - T_base) * (T - T_base) / (T_opt1 - T_base)
- Optimal: TT = T - T_base
- AboveOpt: TT = (T_max - T) / (T_max - T_opt2) * (T_opt2 - T_base)
- Description: Calculates daily thermal time accumulation based on a tri-linear temperature response function.

3.2 DEVELOPMENT RATE
Rate = Daily_TT * TempFactor * PhotoperiodFactor * StressFactor
- Description: The daily progress towards the next developmental stage is driven by thermal time, modulated by environmental factors.

3.3 STAGE PROGRESS
Progress = Accumulated_GDD / Required_GDD
- Description: A stage is complete when the accumulated thermal time equals the requirement for that stage.

3.4 PHOTOPERIOD FACTOR (REPRODUCTIVE)
Factor = 1.0 + (Daylength - CriticalPhotoperiod) * Slope
- Description: For long-day plants like lettuce, daylengths exceeding a critical threshold accelerate reproductive development (bolting).

3.5 STRESS ACCELERATION FACTOR
Factor = 1.0 + StressAcceleration * (1.0 - StressLevel)
- Description: Some stresses (e.g., drought, heat) can accelerate development as an escape mechanism.

3.6 BOLTING RISK (PHOTOPERIOD)
Risk += min(MaxRisk, (Daylength - Threshold) / Divisor)
- Description: Bolting risk increases when the daylength exceeds a specific threshold.

3.7 BOLTING RISK (TEMPERATURE)
Risk += min(MaxRisk, (T - Threshold) / Divisor)
- Description: Bolting risk increases when the temperature exceeds a specific threshold.

3.8 BOLTING RISK (CUMULATIVE STRESS)
Risk += SustainedStressRisk if (AvgTemp > T_thresh AND AvgPhotoperiod > P_thresh)
- Description: Sustained periods of both high temperature and long days significantly increase bolting risk.

3.9 BOLTING RISK (MATURITY)
Risk += min(MaxRisk, (NodeNumber - 10) * Factor)
- Description: As the plant matures (indicated by node number), its propensity to bolt increases.

3.10 THERMAL REQUIREMENT FOR LATE LEAVES
Requirement = BaseRequirement * (1.3 or 1.6)
- Description: The thermal time required to produce a new leaf (phyllochron) increases for later leaves.

3.11 NODE NUMBER UPDATE
NodeNumber = int(V_stage.value[1:])
- Description: The node number is derived directly from the vegetative stage identifier (e.g., 'V7' -> 7 nodes).

3.12 HEAD FORMATION CAPABILITY
CanFormHead = (NodeNumber >= HeadFormationRequirement)
- Description: Lettuce can only begin forming a head after it has produced a minimum number of leaves.

3.13 STAGE TRANSITION
- Description: The model progresses through a defined sequence of growth stages (e.g., V1 -> V2 -> ... -> HI -> HM).

3.14 VERNALIZATION
- Description: Tracks accumulation of cold days, although not typically required for lettuce.

3.15 GET THERMAL REQUIREMENT
Requirement = ThermalRequirements[transition_key]
- Description: Retrieves the specific GDD requirement for each developmental stage transition from a configuration table.

4. LEAF DEVELOPMENT MODEL (12 equations)
File: src/models/leaf_development.py
=====================================================

4.1 THERMAL TIME FOR LEAF DEVELOPMENT
- Description: Uses the same cardinal temperature model as phenology (see 3.1) to drive leaf appearance and expansion.

4.2 EFFECTIVE THERMAL TIME
Effective_TT = Daily_TT * AppearanceFactor
- Description: The thermal time driving leaf appearance is adjusted by stress factors.

4.3 PHYLLOCHRON ADJUSTMENT
TT_required = BasePhyllochron * (1.3 or 1.6)
- Description: The thermal time required between successive leaves increases as the plant gets larger.

4.4 COMBINED STRESS FACTORS
AppearanceFactor = WaterFactor * TempFactor
ExpansionFactor = WaterFactor * N_Factor * TempFactor
- Description: Leaf appearance is primarily affected by water and temperature stress, while expansion is also sensitive to nitrogen availability.

4.5 LEAF POSITION FACTOR
- Early: Factor = 0.4 + (V_stage - 1) * 0.15
- Late: Factor = max(0.6, 1.0 - (V_stage - 15) * 0.04)
- Description: Determines the maximum potential size of a leaf based on its position on the stem. Middle leaves are the largest.

4.6 CELL DIVISION & EXPANSION RATES
DivisionRate = BaseRate * TempFactor
ExpansionRate = BaseRate * WaterFactor * N_Factor
- Description: Leaf area growth is modeled based on the underlying processes of cell division (temperature-dependent) and cell expansion (turgor- and nutrient-dependent).

4.7 DAILY AREA INCREASE
DailyIncrease = RemainingPotential * CombinedGrowthFactor
- Description: The increase in a leaf's area is proportional to its remaining growth potential, creating a sigmoidal growth curve for each leaf.

4.8 LEAF COHORT TRACKING
- Description: The model tracks each individual leaf (or cohort of leaves) from emergence to senescence.

4.9 TOTAL LEAF AREA
TotalArea = Σ(Cohort_i.current_area)
- Description: The total leaf area of the plant is the sum of the areas of all living leaf cohorts.

4.10 LEAF AREA INDEX (LAI)
LAI = (TotalArea * PlantCount) / GroundArea
- Description: LAI is calculated from the total leaf area of all plants divided by the ground area they occupy.

4.11 SPECIFIC LEAF AREA (SLA)
SLA = TotalLeafArea_cm2 / TotalLeafBiomass_g
- Description: A dynamic output of the model, representing the area per unit of leaf biomass.

4.12 SENESCENCE RATE
SenescenceRate = max(0.02, AgeFactor * 0.04 + StressSenescence)
- Description: The rate at which a leaf begins to senesce is determined by its age and the level of environmental stress.

5. CANOPY ARCHITECTURE MODEL (12 equations)
File: src/models/canopy_architecture.py
=====================================================

5.1 BEER'S LAW (LIGHT EXTINCTION)
I = I₀ * exp(-k * LAI)
- Description: Models the exponential decay of light intensity as it passes through the canopy.

5.2 LIGHT EXTINCTION COEFFICIENT (k)
k = G(θ) / cos(β)
- Description: The extinction coefficient depends on the leaf angle distribution (G(θ)) and the solar elevation angle (β).

5.3 SUNLIT & SHADED LAI FRACTIONS
f_sunlit = (1 - exp(-k * LAI)) / (k * LAI)
f_shaded = 1 - f_sunlit
- Description: Partitions the total LAI into a sunlit fraction (receiving direct and diffuse light) and a shaded fraction (receiving only diffuse and scattered light).

5.4 MULTI-LAYER CANOPY
- Description: The model divides the canopy into multiple layers, calculating light interception and photosynthesis for each layer independently.

5.5 LEAF AREA DISTRIBUTION
- Description: The total LAI is distributed vertically among the canopy layers, often following a beta distribution, with the highest leaf area density in the middle layers.

5.6 LIGHT INTERCEPTION
I_intercepted = I_incident - I_transmitted
- Description: The amount of light intercepted by the canopy is the incident light minus the light that passes through to the ground.

5.7 LAYER-BY-LAYER LIGHT CALCULATION
I_layer = I_incident * exp(-k * LAI_cumulative_above)
- Description: The light available at the top of any given layer is calculated based on the cumulative LAI of all layers above it.

5.8 CANOPY TEMPERATURE PROFILE
T_layer = T_air - GradientFactor * CumulativeLAI
- Description: A simple model for the temperature gradient within the canopy, where lower layers are cooler.

5.9 ROW & SPACING EFFECTS
GroundCoverage = (PlantWidth² / (RowSpacing * PlantSpacing))
- Description: Accounts for the effect of plant spacing on ground coverage and total light interception by the community of plants.

5.10 TOTAL ABSORBED PPFD
Absorbed_PPFD = Σ(PPFD_avg_layer * LAI_layer)
- Description: Total photosynthetically active photon flux density absorbed by the canopy is the sum of absorption in each layer.

5.11 CANOPY PHOTOSYNTHESIS
Canopy_A = Σ(Photosynthesis_layer * LAI_layer)
- Description: Total canopy carbon assimilation is the sum of the photosynthetic rates of each layer, scaled by the LAI of that layer.

5.12 CLUMPING INDEX
k_effective = k * ClumpingIndex
- Description: Adjusts the extinction coefficient to account for the non-random, clumped distribution of leaves in real canopies.

6. ROOT SYSTEM MODEL (20+ equations)
File: src/models/root_system_model.py
=====================================================

6.1 ROOT COHORT TRACKING
- Description: The model simulates root growth by creating and tracking cohorts of roots with similar age and properties.

6.2 ROOT SURFACE AREA
SurfaceArea = π * diameter * length
- Description: Calculates the surface area of a root cohort, which is critical for uptake calculations.

6.3 ROOT ACTIVITY FACTOR (AGE)
Activity = 0.5^(Age_days / HalfLife_days)
- Description: The metabolic and uptake activity of a root cohort declines with age, following a half-life decay function.

6.4 UPTAKE CAPACITY
Capacity = SurfaceArea * ActivityFactor * BaseUptakeRate
- Description: The potential nutrient uptake of a root cohort depends on its surface area, age-related activity, and an intrinsic uptake rate.

6.5 SPECIFIC ROOT LENGTH (SRL)
SRL = Length / Biomass
- Description: A key root trait representing the length of root produced per unit of biomass invested (cm/g).

6.6 ROOT LENGTH DENSITY (RLD)
RLD = TotalLength / ZoneVolume
- Description: The total length of roots within a given volume of the root zone (cm/cm³).

6.7 ROOT SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE (SYSTEM-DEPENDENT)
- NFT: Elongated, mat-like structure.
- DWC: Dense, fibrous structure suspended in solution.
- Aeroponics: Highly branched, "fuzzy" structure in the air.
- Description: The model adjusts growth parameters (e.g., branching, length) based on the hydroponic system type.

6.8 ROOT ZONE LAYERS
- Description: The root zone is divided into spatial layers, each with its own environmental conditions (temp, O2, etc.) and root population.

6.9 NEW ROOT GENERATION
NewGrowth = BaseGrowthRate * N_Factor * Water_Factor * Temp_Factor
- Description: The creation of new roots is driven by the plant's growth potential, adjusted by environmental and nutrient stress factors.

6.10 ROOT TURNOVER
- Description: Simulates the death and decomposition of old root cohorts based on type-specific turnover rates.

6.11 UPTAKE RATE ADJUSTMENT (ENVIRONMENT)
AdjustedRate = BaseRate * TempFactor * FlowFactor * O2_Factor * pH_Factor
- Description: The base nutrient uptake rate is modified by the conditions in the root zone layer.

6.12 MICHAELIS-MENTEN UPTAKE KINETICS
UptakeRate = (Vmax * Concentration) / (Km + Concentration)
- Description: Models the relationship between nutrient concentration in the solution and the rate of uptake by the roots.

6.13 NUTRIENT COMPETITION
InhibitionFactor = 1 / (1 + [Inhibitor] / Ki)
- Description: Accounts for the competitive inhibition of uptake between similar ions (e.g., NH4+ and K+).

6.14 PH EFFECT ON UPTAKE
- Description: A pH-dependent factor adjusts uptake rates, as different nutrients are more or less available at different pH levels.

6.15 EFFECTIVE ROOT SURFACE AREA
EffectiveArea = Σ(Area_type * Effectiveness_type)
- Description: Calculates the total effective surface area for uptake by weighting the surface area of different root types (fine, medium, coarse) by their relative effectiveness.

6.16 ROOT GROWTH POTENTIAL (SPATIAL)
Potential = AuxinGradient * NutrientSignal * O2_Effect * CompetitionEffect
- Description: Determines where new roots are likely to grow based on a combination of internal hormonal signals (auxin) and local environmental conditions.

6.17 ROOT HEALTH SCORE
- Description: A composite score based on mass, growth rate, efficiency, and senescence to provide an overall assessment of root system health.

6.18 SOLUTION CONTACT FRACTION
- Description: A parameter specific to hydroponic systems that defines the fraction of the root system in direct contact with the nutrient solution.

6.19 ROOT RESPIRATION HEAT
- Description: A small amount of heat generated by the metabolic activity of the roots.

6.20 ROOT DIAMETER DISTRIBUTION
Diameter = mean + std_dev * random_normal()
- Description: The diameter for new root cohorts is generated from a normal distribution specific to the root type (fine, medium, coarse).

7. NITROGEN BALANCE MODEL (25+ equations)
File: src/models/nitrogen_balance.py
=====================================================

7.1 MICHAELIS-MENTEN UPTAKE KINETICS
Rate = (Vmax * [N]) / (Km + [N])
- Description: Models the primary mechanism of nitrogen uptake from the solution.

7.2 ENVIRONMENTAL EFFECT ON UPTAKE
Rate_adj = Rate * TempFactor * WaterFactor * RootHealth * pH_Factor
- Description: The potential uptake rate is adjusted by various environmental factors.

7.3 ACTUAL UPTAKE
Uptake = Rate_adj * RootMass * ExplorationFactor
- Description: The total nitrogen taken up by the plant per day.

7.4 NITROGEN DEMAND
Demand = GrowthRate * Target_N_Concentration
- Description: The amount of nitrogen required to support new growth at an optimal N concentration.

7.5 TARGET N CONCENTRATION ADJUSTMENT
- Veg: Target = OptimalN * 1.1
- Repro: Target = OptimalN * 1.2
- Description: The target nitrogen concentration for new tissues varies with the developmental stage.

7.6 WEIGHTED N ALLOCATION
Allocated_N = Available_N * (Demand_organ * Priority_organ) / Σ(Demand_i * Priority_i)
- Description: Available nitrogen is partitioned to different organs based on their relative demand and a system of priorities that changes with growth stage.

7.7 NITROGEN POOLS
- Structural: Proteins, cell walls.
- Metabolic: Enzymes, chlorophyll.
- Storage: Amino acids, vegetative storage proteins.
- Transport: Mobile N compounds in xylem and phloem.
- Description: Total nitrogen within an organ is divided into functional pools with different characteristics.

7.8 STRESS-INDUCED REMOBILIZATION
Remobilized_N = StoragePool * StressRate * OverallStress
- Description: During stress, nitrogen is moved from storage pools to metabolic pools to support survival.

7.9 SENESCENCE-INDUCED REMOBILIZATION
Remobilized_N = MetabolicPool * SenescenceRate * RemobilizationEfficiency
- Description: As tissues senesce, a fraction of their nitrogen is salvaged and moved to other parts of the plant.

7.10 ORGAN N STRESS LEVEL
Stress = 1 - (CurrentN - CriticalN) / (OptimalN - CriticalN)
- Description: An index (0-1) quantifying the level of nitrogen stress within an organ based on its current N concentration relative to critical and optimal levels.

7.11 PLANT N STRESS
OverallStress = Σ(OrganStress_i * OrganWeight_i) / Σ(OrganWeight_i)
- Description: The overall plant N stress is the biomass-weighted average of the stress levels of individual organs.

7.12 NITROGEN USE EFFICIENCY (NUE)
NUE = TotalBiomass / TotalPlantN
- Description: A measure of how much biomass is produced per unit of nitrogen in the plant.

7.13 NITROGEN BALANCE
Balance = Available_N - TotalGrowthDemand
- Description: The daily balance between available nitrogen (uptake + remobilization) and the demand for new growth.

7.14 POOL DISTRIBUTION
- Description: Newly acquired nitrogen is partitioned into the different functional pools (structural, metabolic, etc.) based on defined fractions.

7.15-7.25: Additional equations in the model cover competitive inhibition, root activity, allocation efficiency, demand satisfaction, and cumulative tracking, providing a comprehensive simulation of N dynamics.

8. NUTRIENT MOBILITY & CONCENTRATION (20+ equations)
File: src/models/nutrient_models.py
=====================================================

8.1 ELECTRICAL CONDUCTIVITY (EC) CALCULATION
EC = Σ(coeff_i * concentration_i)
- Description: Estimates the total EC of the nutrient solution by summing the contributions of each major ion.

8.2 EC-BASED UPTAKE MODIFIER
Modifier = 1.0 - (EC_ratio - 1.0) * Sensitivity
- Description: High EC reduces overall nutrient uptake efficiency due to osmotic stress.

8.3 XYLEM TRANSPORT CAPACITY
XylemCap = WaterFlux * XylemCapacity * TranspirationCoupling
- Description: The capacity of the xylem to transport nutrients is linked to the water flux (transpiration stream).

8.4 PHLOEM TRANSPORT CAPACITY
PhloemCap = AssimilateFlux * PhloemCapacity
- Description: The capacity of the phloem to transport nutrients is linked to the flow of sugars from source to sink tissues.

8.5 TEMPERATURE EFFECT ON TRANSPORT (Q10)
TempFactor = Q10^((T - 25.0) / 10.0)
- Description: The rate of nutrient transport is temperature-dependent.

8.6 SINK STRENGTH ADJUSTMENT
AdjustedDemand = Demand * OrganSinkStrength
- Description: The demand of an organ is weighted by its intrinsic "sink strength," which varies with growth stage.

8.7 MOBILITY CLASSIFICATION ADJUSTMENT
- Description: The demand of an organ is further adjusted based on the mobility of the specific nutrient (e.g., demand for immobile nutrients like Calcium is less flexible).

8.8 TOTAL NUTRIENT SUPPLY
TotalSupply = BaseSupply + StressSupply + SenescenceSupply
- Description: The total supply of a nutrient available for redistribution is the sum of what's available from base storage, stress-induced mobilization, and senescence.

8.9 TRANSPORT EFFICIENCY
TransportEfficiency = min(1.0, TotalSupply / TotalDemand)
- Description: The efficiency of transport is limited by the ratio of supply to demand. If demand exceeds supply, not all demand can be met.

8.10-8.20+: The model includes detailed equations for organ-specific nutrient pools (metabolic, storage, buffer), transport fluxes between organs, and redistribution thresholds, creating a complete in-plant nutrient economy.

9. PH MODEL (10 equations)
File: src/models/ph_model.py
=====================================================

9.1 HENDERSON-HASSELBALCH EQUATION
pH = pKa + log([A⁻] / [HA])
- Description: The core equation for calculating pH in a buffer system, used for both carbonate and phosphate buffers.

9.2 PHOSPHATE SPECIATION
- α_H3PO4, α_H2PO4⁻, α_HPO4²⁻, α_PO4³⁻ = f(pH, pKa1, pKa2, pKa3)
- Description: Calculates the fraction of each of the four phosphate species present at a given pH. This is critical as plants primarily take up H₂PO₄⁻.

9.3 NUTRIENT UPTAKE PH EFFECT
ΔpH = (N_uptake_effect) + (P_uptake_effect)
- Description: Calculates the change in solution pH caused by the net charge balance of ion uptake. Nitrate uptake acidifies, while ammonium uptake alkalinizes.

9.4 BUFFER CAPACITY
β = d(Base) / d(pH)
- Description: Models the solution's resistance to pH change. It's a function of the concentrations and pKa values of the buffering agents (carbonates, phosphates).

9.5 PH-DEPENDENT NUTRIENT SOLUBILITY
- Max_PO4 = f(pH)
- Max_Fe = f(pH)
- Description: Models the precipitation of nutrients like phosphate and iron at high pH, reducing their availability.

9.6 CALCIUM-PHOSPHATE PRECIPITATION (Ksp)
Ksp = [Ca²⁺]³ * [PO₄³⁻]²
- Description: Determines if calcium phosphate will precipitate based on the ion activity product compared to the solubility product constant (Ksp).

9.7 PH CONTROL SYSTEM
Dose = (pH_error - Deadband) * ProportionalFactor
- Description: Simulates an automated pH controller, calculating the dose of acid or base needed to correct deviations from the target pH.

9.8 TEMPERATURE CORRECTION FOR PKA
pKa_corrected = pKa - CorrectionFactor * (T - 25)
- Description: Adjusts the pKa values of the buffer systems based on temperature.

9.9 NATURAL PH DRIFT
- Description: An empirical factor to account for pH changes due to other biological processes, like root exudates or microbial activity.

9.10 IONIC STRENGTH EFFECT
- Description: The model can adjust pKa values based on the ionic strength of the solution, although this is often simplified in hydroponic models.

10. TEMPERATURE STRESS MODEL (15 equations)
File: src/models/temperature_stress.py
=====================================================

10.1 STRESS CLASSIFICATION
- Description: Classifies the current temperature as OPTIMAL, COLD, HEAT, or FROST based on defined thresholds.

10.2 BASE STRESS LEVEL CALCULATION
- Mild: Stress = 0.3 * (T - T_opt) / (T_mild - T_opt)
- Moderate: Stress = 0.3 + 0.4 * (T - T_mild) / (T_severe - T_mild)
- Severe: Stress = 0.7 + 0.3 * (T - T_severe) / (T_lethal - T_severe)
- Description: A piecewise linear function to determine the initial stress level (0-1) based on how far the temperature deviates from the optimal range.

10.3 ACCLIMATION UPDATE
Acclimation_new = Acclimation_old + Rate * (Target - Acclimation_old)
- Description: The plant's level of acclimation to heat or cold gradually adjusts towards a target level based on recent temperature exposure.

10.4 ACCLIMATION EFFECT
Stress_adj = BaseStress * (1 - AcclimationLevel * 0.4)
- Description: The calculated stress level is reduced by the plant's current level of acclimation.

10.5 STRESS MEMORY
Memory = WeightedAvg(RecentStresses) * MemoryStrength
- Description: Past stress events have a lingering effect, calculated as a weighted average of recent stress levels.

10.6 PROCESS-SPECIFIC STRESS FACTORS
ProcessFactor = 1.0 - FinalStress * Sensitivity
- Description: The final, adjusted stress level is translated into a performance reduction factor for specific biological processes (e.g., photosynthesis, growth), each with its own sensitivity.

10.7 DAMAGE ACCUMULATION
Damage += (StressLevel - DamageThreshold) * DamageRate
- Description: If stress exceeds a certain threshold, cellular damage begins to accumulate.

10.8 DAMAGE RECOVERY
Damage = max(0, Damage - RecoveryRate)
- Description: During periods of low stress, the plant can repair accumulated damage.

10.9 FROST DAMAGE
FrostDamage += FrostRate / 24.0
- Description: A specific function to calculate damage during freezing conditions.

10.10 OVERALL PROCESS FACTOR
Overall = Σ(ProcessFactor_i * Weight_i)
- Description: An overall stress factor is calculated as a weighted average of the impacts on key processes.

10.11-10.15: The model includes additional logic for acclimation decay, stress duration tracking, and combining different damage types into a single impact factor.

11. INTEGRATED STRESS MODEL (15 equations)

11.1 ACUTE STRESS
AcuteStress = (CurrentLevel / Threshold)^2
- Description: Calculates the immediate impact of a stressor, often with a non-linear response.

11.2 CHRONIC STRESS
ChronicStress = WeightedAvg(StressHistory)
- Description: Calculates the long-term impact of a persistent stress, using an exponentially decaying weighted average of past stress levels.

11.3 STRESS INTERACTION (MULTIPLICATIVE)
Combined = Stress1 * Stress2
- Description: When two stresses act independently, their combined effect is multiplicative.

11.4 STRESS INTERACTION (SYNERGISTIC)
Combined = (Stress1 + Stress2) * Factor
- Description: When two stresses amplify each other (e.g., heat and water stress), their combined effect is greater than the sum of their individual effects.

11.5 COMBINED PROCESS STRESS
ProcessFactor = min(IndividualFactors) - InteractionPenalty + AcclimationBonus - DamagePenalty
- Description: The final stress factor for a given biological process is determined by the most limiting individual stress, adjusted for interactions, acclimation, and accumulated damage.

11.6 OVERALL STRESS FACTOR
OverallStress = np.prod(StressFactors)
- Description: The overall plant stress is often calculated as the product of all individual stress factors (temperature, water, nutrient, etc.).

11.7 STRESS SEVERITY CLASSIFICATION
- Description: The model classifies the overall stress level into categories like 'mild', 'moderate', 'severe', or 'critical'.

11.8 DOMINANT STRESS IDENTIFICATION
- Description: Identifies the top one or two stressors that are having the largest negative impact on the plant.

11.9-11.15: The model includes detailed state tracking for each stress type, including its history, acclimation level, damage, and recovery progress, providing a comprehensive view of the plant's status.

12. ROOT ZONE TEMPERATURE (RZT) MODEL (8 equations)
File: src/models/root_zone_temperature.py
=====================================================

12.1 OPTIMAL RZT
RZT_opt = T_air + Offset
- Description: The optimal root zone temperature is typically a few degrees warmer than the air temperature.

12.2 GROWTH FACTOR (BELOW OPTIMUM)
Factor = BaseFactor + (RZT_opt - RZT_current) * LinearSlope
- Description: Plant growth increases linearly as RZT approaches the optimum from the cold side.

12.3 GROWTH FACTOR (ABOVE OPTIMUM)
Factor = BaseFactor - (RZT_current - RZT_opt) * DeclineSlope
- Description: Plant growth declines rapidly when RZT exceeds the optimum.

12.4 NUTRIENT UPTAKE FACTOR
- Description: Nutrient uptake has its own response curve to RZT, which is generally broader than the growth response.

12.5 WATER UPTAKE FACTOR
- Description: Water uptake is also affected by RZT, influencing root hydraulic conductivity.

12.6 ROOT METABOLISM FACTOR
- Description: Root respiration and other metabolic activities are highly sensitive to RZT, generally following a Q10 response.

12.7 THERMAL DYNAMICS
New_RZT = Prev_RZT + (Target_RZT - Prev_RZT) * (1 - exp(-dt / τ))
- Description: Models the thermal inertia of the hydroponic solution. The RZT does not change instantaneously but approaches a target temperature (influenced by air temp, heaters, etc.) over a characteristic time (τ).

12.8 HEAT TRANSFER RATE
HeatRate = U * A * (T_solution - T_air)
- Description: A simplified heat transfer equation to estimate the heating or cooling load required to maintain the target RZT.

13. ENVIRONMENTAL CONTROL SYSTEM (30+ equations)
File: src/models/environmental_control.py
=====================================================

13.1 SATURATION VAPOR PRESSURE (es)
es = 0.6108 * exp(17.27 * T / (T + 237.3))
- Description: Calculates the saturation vapor pressure of water at a given temperature.

13.2 VAPOR PRESSURE DEFICIT (VPD)
VPD = es * (1 - RH / 100)
- Description: The difference between the amount of moisture the air can hold and the amount it currently holds. A key driver for transpiration.

13.3 OPTIMAL HUMIDITY CALCULATION
RH_optimal = ( (es - VPD_target) / es ) * 100
- Description: Calculates the relative humidity required to achieve a target VPD at a given temperature.

13.4 CO2 PHOTOSYNTHESIS ENHANCEMENT
Enhancement = (Vmax * [CO2]) / (Km + [CO2])
- Description: A Michaelis-Menten function that models the enhancement of photosynthesis at elevated CO2 levels.

13.5 VPD STRESS FACTOR
- Low VPD: Factor = 1.0 - (VPD_optimal - VPD_current) * Sensitivity
- High VPD: Factor = 1.0 - (VPD_current - VPD_optimal) * Sensitivity
- Description: Calculates a stress factor when VPD is outside the optimal range, affecting both photosynthesis and transpiration.

13.6 PID CONTROLLER
Output = Kp*error + Ki*∫(error dt) + Kd*(d(error)/dt)
- Description: The core equation for Proportional-Integral-Derivative controllers used for precise control of humidity, temperature, and CO2.

13.7-13.30+: The model contains numerous other equations for calculating equipment run times, energy consumption, CO2 injection rates, ventilation requirements, and operating costs, forming a complete simulation of a controlled environment agriculture (CEA) system.

14. MAIN SIMULATOR LOGIC (40+ equations)
File: src/cropgro_hydroponic_simulator.py
=====================================================

14.1 PLANT DENSITY
PlantDensity = PlantCount / SystemArea
- Description: Number of plants per square meter.

14.2 PPFD CONVERSION
PPFD = SolarRadiation_MJ_day * ConversionFactor
- Description: Converts daily solar radiation (in MJ/m²/day) to an average photosynthetically active photon flux density (in μmol/m²/s).

14.3 NET CARBON ASSIMILATION
NetCarbon = CanopyPhotosynthesis - TotalRespiration
- Description: The net carbon gained by the plant per day.

14.4 BIOMASS GROWTH
AvailableBiomass = NetCarbon / (C_to_Biomass_Ratio * (1 + GrowthRespirationFraction))
- Description: Converts net carbon gain into potential new biomass, accounting for the carbon cost of growth respiration.

14.5 BIOMASS ALLOCATION
Growth_organ = AvailableBiomass * AllocationFraction_organ
- Description: The total new biomass is partitioned to different organs (leaves, stems, roots) based on fractions that change with the plant's developmental stage.

14.6 FUNCTIONAL BALANCE ALLOCATION
- Description: A more advanced allocation scheme where partitioning fractions are dynamically adjusted based on which resource (light or nutrients) is most limiting. More carbon is allocated to roots under N stress, and to leaves under light limitation.

14.7 WATER UPTAKE (PENMAN-MONTEITH)
ETo = f(SolarRad, Temp, Humidity, Wind)
ETc = ETo * Kc
- Description: Calculates crop evapotranspiration (ETc) based on reference evapotranspiration (ETo) and a crop-specific coefficient (Kc) that varies with LAI.

14.8 WATER UPTAKE (HYDRAULIC)
Uptake = K_hydraulic * (Ψ_solution - Ψ_leaf)
- Description: A more mechanistic water uptake model based on the water potential gradient between the nutrient solution and the leaves, and the hydraulic conductance of the plant.

14.9 NUTRIENT CONCENTRATION UPDATE
NewConc = OldConc - (TotalUptake_mg / TankVolume_L)
- Description: Updates the nutrient concentration in the hydroponic solution based on plant uptake.

14.10 PH UPDATE (NUTRIENT UPTAKE)
ΔpH = (NO3_uptake * AcidFactor) + (NH4_uptake * BaseFactor)
- Description: The pH of the solution changes based on the charge imbalance created by the uptake of different forms of nitrogen.

14.11 OVERALL STRESS FACTOR
OverallStress = TempFactor * WaterFactor * LightFactor * N_Factor * SalinityFactor * ...
- Description: The final factor limiting growth is the product of all individual environmental stress factors.

14.12-14.40+: The main simulator is the orchestrator, containing dozens of equations that connect the outputs of one model to the inputs of another, manage state variables (like biomass pools, LAI, nutrient levels), and calculate summary outputs (like WUE).
